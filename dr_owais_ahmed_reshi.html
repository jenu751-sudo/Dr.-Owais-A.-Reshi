About the Doctor

Healing with Expertise & Empathy

Dr. Owais Ahmad Reshi is a qualified Internist specializing in General Medicine, bringing over 3 years of hands-on clinical experience treating a diverse range of patients. Dr. Reshi is equally adept at handling adult and pediatric patients, making him a trusted one-stop physician for entire families. He is especially recognized for his proficiency in diagnosing and treating anemia and blood-related conditions.
